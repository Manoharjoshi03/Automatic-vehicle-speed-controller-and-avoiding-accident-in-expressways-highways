# Automatic-vehicle-speed-controller-and-avoiding-accident-in-expressways-highways

As we know, Indian roads are on a path of rapid development and the prime example of this are the
highspeed expressways that are being constructed which links major cities and provides the facility
to commute between the cities in less time.
This nature of expressways open possibilities for major accidents as speed limits are usually triple digits and many people disregarding the limits. 
This can be understood my taking Bangalore-Mysore expressway as an example. This expressway recorded
296 accidents out of which 132 resulted in fatality in a time frame of January 2023 to July 2023. This is a major issue as more and more expressways 
are being constructed and the accidents will only continue to rise.
The main cause of the accidents is found to be reckless driving and excessive speeding. Hence our
project aims to solve this particular issue by implementing technology in order to make sure drivers
follow lane discipline and maintain the speed limits.
We are going to make use of electronic sensors and transceivers with the help of AI/ML in order to govern the speed of the engine with respect to the 
lane in which the vehicle is travelling on. This ensures that the speed limit is being followed by not allowing the engine to exceed the limit.
We are also going to implement an automatic fining system that puts fine on a vehicle travelling in
the fast lane at a very slow pace even after being alerted to switch lanes to let the fast traffic to pass by. 
We aim to make expressways safe for people to travel by making sure the rules are being
followed.
